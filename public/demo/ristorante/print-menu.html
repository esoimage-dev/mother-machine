    How to print

    Print or save PDF opens the print window with the A4 sheets. Save it as a PDF for the print shop, or print it straight away.

    In the print window or at the shop: A4, double-sided, flip on the short edge, scale 100%, no margins. Fold each sheet in half and nest sheet 2 inside sheet 1.

    Guides show the area dishes must stay in, the price column, the last line, and dashed spaces where another dish would still fit. They never print.
