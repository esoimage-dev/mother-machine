Where we come from

We run a business too.

Mother Machine started inside a restaurant and a food truck we run ourselves, building the machine they run on: bookings, orders, menus, deposits, the screen in the kitchen.

So we know what a Friday night feels like when the system stops. We build for the people who use it on a Monday morning, not for a screenshot.

Five rules we keep
  1. 01Same hands, start to finish.

    Design and code are not two departments passing files. One team holds the whole thing.

  2. 02Inside and outside, together.

    The system your team uses and the brand your clients see are designed as one machine.

  3. 03One number to move.

    We agree on it before we start, and we tell you the truth about it after.

  4. 04Built to be run.

    Simple to use on a busy day, simple to change when the business grows.

  5. 05We stay.

    After launch someone always answers. Usually the people who built it.
